My experience volunteering at BSidesNYC 2025

Published on November 2025

My first conference of this scale was an incredible experience that I won't forget anytime soon. Volunteering at BSidesNYC 2025 gave me the opportunity to immerse myself in the cybersecurity community and contribute to making the event successful.

The Experience

Walking into the venue on day one, I was immediately struck by the energy and passion of the cybersecurity professionals gathered there. From seasoned veterans to newcomers like myself, everyone was eager to learn, share knowledge, and network.

What I Learned

  • Networking is invaluable: The connections I made will be instrumental in my career growth
  • Community matters: The cybersecurity community is incredibly welcoming and supportive
  • Continuous learning: There's always something new to discover in this field
  • Event management: Behind the scenes, organizing a conference is a massive undertaking

Key Takeaways

As a volunteer, I was responsible for helping manage the CTF (Capture The Flag) competition and assisting attendees throughout the event. This hands-on experience gave me insights into:

  • How large-scale security events are coordinated
  • The importance of community in cybersecurity
  • Real-world challenges that security professionals face
  • The value of mentorship and peer support

Networking Highlights

One of the most rewarding aspects was meeting professionals from various backgrounds and companies. I had the opportunity to discuss:

  • Career paths in cybersecurity
  • Different specializations and roles
  • Industry trends and emerging threats
  • Open-source security projects

Moving Forward

This experience has reinforced my passion for cybersecurity and my commitment to being an active member of the community. I'm excited to apply what I learned and continue contributing to initiatives like BSidesNYC.

"The best way to predict the future is to create it." - And that's exactly what the cybersecurity community is doing, one conference at a time.

What's Next?

I'm looking forward to:

  • Attending more security conferences
  • Contributing to open-source security projects
  • Mentoring others interested in cybersecurity
  • Continuing to deepen my expertise in cloud security and threat detection

Thanks to the BSidesNYC organizers for putting on an amazing event, and to everyone I met there who made it truly special!
